Notary Public Checklist & Client Guide

Mandatory Requirements for Notary Attestation at Khamgaon Chamber:

  • Original Identification Proof:
    Aadhaar Card, Passport, Election Voter ID, or PAN Card of the signatory.
  • Physical Presence of Deponent:
    The person signing the affidavit or agreement must be personally present before the Government Notary Public.
  • Non-Judicial Stamp Paper:
    Affidavits, Power of Attorney, or declarations must be printed on the appropriate value stamp paper as mandated under the Maharashtra Stamp Act.
  • Recent Photographs:
    2 passport-sized color photographs for Power of Attorney, Property Agreements, or specific statutory affidavits.
